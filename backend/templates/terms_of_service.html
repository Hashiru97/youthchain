What YouthChain is

YouthChain connects job seekers and employers in Sierra Leone, and lets job seekers build a portable record of their skills, credentials, and work history. YouthChain provides the platform — the accounts, the listings, the messaging, the credential registry — but is not a party to any employment relationship, gig arrangement, or hiring decision made through it.

Credentials and verification — what we do and don't guarantee

When you upload a credential, YouthChain records a cryptographic fingerprint of that exact file on a blockchain, timestamped, tamper-evident. That is a real, technical guarantee: the file you see today is the same one originally uploaded, unaltered.

What YouthChain does not guarantee: that the issuer, title, or year you entered are accurate. Those are entered by the credential holder, the same as on a paper certificate — YouthChain does not independently contact the named school or employer to confirm they issued it. "Verified" on this platform means this file has not been altered since upload, not this institution confirms issuing this credential.

Employers and other users are responsible for their own reliance on any credential, rating, job listing, or other content submitted by another user. YouthChain provides tools to report suspected fraud (see below) and reviews reports it receives, but does not guarantee the truthfulness of user-submitted content and is not liable for decisions made in reliance on it.

Reporting and enforcement

  • Any user can report a job listing, employer, rating, or credential they believe is fraudulent or violates these terms.
  • YouthChain admins review reports and can suspend accounts, revoke credentials, or remove listings found to violate these terms.
  • A suspended user or employer can appeal that decision — see the app for the current process.
  • Reporting and enforcement are best-effort, not a guarantee that every violation will be caught or caught before harm occurs.
