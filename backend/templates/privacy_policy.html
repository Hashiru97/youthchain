Who can see your data

  • You, always, via the app.
  • An employer you apply to sees your application, CV, and any messages you send them for that application — nothing else.
  • YouthChain admins can see platform data to operate, moderate, and secure the service, and to review employer verification documents.
  • YouthChain does not sell personal data to third parties.

Your choices

  • You must actively accept this policy (a required checkbox) before registering.
  • You can contact YouthChain to request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to records YouthChain is legally required to retain (e.g. issued credentials that other parties may already have verified).

Questions

This platform is under active development. If you have questions about your data, contact the YouthChain team through the channel where you obtained this app.
